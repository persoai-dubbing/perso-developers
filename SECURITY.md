# Security Policy

We take the security of the Perso Developer Portal and the Perso AI APIs seriously. Thank you for taking the time to report vulnerabilities responsibly.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public GitHub issue for sensitive vulnerabilities.** Public issues are visible to everyone and can be exploited before we have time to patch.

Please report sensitive vulnerabilities through one of these private channels:

- **Email:** [security@perso.ai](mailto:security@perso.ai)
- **GitHub:** [Private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/persoai-dubbing/perso-developers/security/advisories/new)

When reporting, please include:

- A clear description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof-of-concept code, requests, or screenshots)
- The affected endpoint, page, or component
- Your assessment of severity, if any
- Whether the issue has been disclosed elsewhere

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement:** within 3 business days of your report.
- **Initial assessment:** within 7 business days, including a severity estimate and expected timeline.
- **Updates:** we'll keep you informed as we investigate and remediate.
- **Credit:** with your permission, we'll credit you in the advisory once the issue is fixed.

## Scope

In scope:

- This repository (`perso-developers`)
- The Perso Developer Portal (`developers.perso.ai`)
- The Perso AI API (`api.perso.ai`)

Out of scope:

- Third-party services we depend on (report to the vendor instead)
- Social engineering, physical attacks, or denial-of-service testing
- Findings from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact
- Issues that require an already-compromised account or device

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Make a good-faith effort to follow this policy
- Avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption
- Report findings privately and give us reasonable time to remediate before any public disclosure

For low-impact, non-sensitive security questions (e.g. headers, CORS, public configuration), you may use the **Security Report** issue template instead.
